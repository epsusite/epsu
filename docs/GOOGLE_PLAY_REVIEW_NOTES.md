# Google Play Review Notes Draft

Use this in Play Console where review context or login instructions are needed.

## Core note

Epsu is an 18+ account-based moderated UGC app for local and school communities. Ordinary users do not see author identities, user profiles, direct messaging, follows, or mentions. Posts are pseudonymous to ordinary users, while backend accountability is retained for moderation and safety enforcement.

The app includes:
- in-app post reporting
- moderator/admin review tools
- removal of violating posts
- temporary 24-hour muting
- user-controlled blocking of post authors from the user's own feed
- in-app account deletion

## Login note

The app is login-gated. Reviewer credentials must be provided and kept active so Google Play can access all app functionality.

## Safety note

Posts are checked against a keyword list before release. Flagged queued posts can be reviewed by moderators before they become visible. Users can report posts in-app and block post authors from their own feed. Moderators and admins can review reports, remove violating posts, and apply temporary 24-hour mutes.
