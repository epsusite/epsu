# Google Play Review Notes Draft

Use this in Play Console where review context or login instructions are needed.

## Core note

Epsu is an anonymous UGC app for local and school communities. Ordinary users do not see author identities, user profiles, direct messaging, follows, or mentions. Interaction is centered on anonymous posts inside moderated communities.

The app includes:
- in-app post reporting
- moderator/admin review tools
- removal of violating posts
- temporary 24-hour muting
- user-controlled blocking of anonymous authors from the user’s own feed
- in-app account deletion

## Login note

The app is login-gated. Reviewer credentials must be provided and kept active so Google Play can access all app functionality.

## Safety note

Posts are checked against a keyword list before release. Flagged queued posts can be reviewed by moderators before they become visible. Users can also report posts in-app and block anonymous authors from their own feed.
