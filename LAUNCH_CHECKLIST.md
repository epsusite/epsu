# Epsu Launch Checklist

## Soft Launch Scope

- Pick `3` schools
- Pick `1` regional Epsu
- Make sure each launch Epsu has at least one real person ready to post

## Contact And Trust

- Use `epsu.site@protonmail.com` as the working support contact
- Put the same contact email in app store listings and legal pages

## Product Readiness

- Test sign up
- Test log in
- Test creating a post
- Test replying to a post
- Test reporting a post
- Test swiping like and dislike
- Test school application flow
- Test account history
- Test export data file
- Test delete account

## Moderation Readiness

- Confirm reports reach mod queue
- Confirm hosts can review school applications
- Confirm action records appear in records
- Confirm worst-users list updates from reports

## Legal And Policy

- Recheck Terms of Service
- Recheck Privacy Policy
- Recheck Community Guidelines
- Confirm app behavior matches those documents

## Launch Operations

- Decide exact beta wording
- Decide where feedback should be sent
- Keep a simple issue list during launch week

## Metrics To Watch

- Active users
- Posts per Epsu
- Replies per Epsu
- Reports per Epsu
- Day 1 return rate
- Day 7 return rate

## Go / No-Go

- At least one school shows real repeat activity
- Moderation load stays manageable
- No major crash or account-loss bug appears
