Community Guidelines

Effective date: April 20, 2026. These are the basic rules for posting and participating on Epsu.

1. No harassment or threats

Do not use Epsu to harass, bully, threaten, intimidate, or target people with abuse.

2. No hate or dehumanization

Hate speech, slurs, extremist praise, or attacks on protected groups are not allowed.

3. No sexual exploitation or child abuse content

Sexual exploitation, non-consensual sexual content, and any child sexual abuse material are forbidden.

4. No doxxing or privacy abuse

Do not reveal private identifying information, contact details, or other information meant to expose or endanger someone.

5. No scams, spam, or impersonation

Do not use Epsu for fraud, phishing, spam, impersonation, or other deceptive behavior.

6. No illegal content or criminal coordination

Do not use Epsu to promote illegal goods, coordinate crime, or distribute malware.

7. Respect community usability

Do not flood communities, repeatedly provoke people in bad faith, or use posting patterns that make an Epsu hard to use.

8. Reporting and review

Users can report posts in the app. Moderators and admins can review reported posts and queued keyword-flagged posts, dismiss reports, remove posts, or mute authors for 24 hours.

Users can also block anonymous authors so future posts from that author stop appearing in their own feed.

9. Consequences

Depending on what happened, Epsu may remove a queued post before release, remove a released post, mute you from an Epsu for 24 hours, reject a school application, or otherwise restrict access where the service allows it.

10. Appeals and contact

If you believe moderation was wrong, contact epsu.site@protonmail.com with enough context to review the case.
