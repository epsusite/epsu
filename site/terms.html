5. Description of Service

Epsu provides an anonymous community platform where users can:

  • Join regional Epsus (city, state, country) or apply to school-based Epsus, with a hard limit of 3 regional Epsus and 3 school Epsus per user.
  • Submit posts that are hidden from ordinary users' identity view, with posting governed by hourly UTC batch limits enforced by backend RPCs. The current Service design allows up to 2 top-level posts and 1 reply per Epsu per hourly cycle.
  • View released posts from Epsus they have access to, with posts automatically removed from the user's feed after reacting to them.
  • Reply to released posts within the same Epsu, with reply-to-post scope enforced by backend RPC validation.
  • React to posts with likes or dislikes, with a progressive auto-deletion threshold based on participation rates and dislike ratios (0.80+ dislike ratio for low participation, 0.51+ for high participation).
  • Report posts for violations of Community Guidelines, with reports reviewed by moderators, hosts, or platform admins as applicable.
  • Block post authors to hide their future posts and replies from their feed, via backend-stored block relationships.
  • For school Epsus: apply for membership with a short answer, with hosts reviewing applications; hosts and moderators have scoped governance tools including report resolution, post removal, Epsu-scoped 24-hour muting, member management, moderator invite creation via QR code/deep link, and moderation records access.
  • For regional Epsus: view country-scoped communities, suggest new regional Epsus, with suggestions reviewed by platform admins before approval.
  • Receive optional push notifications for app events supported by the Service, including moderation-related actions, hourly post-drop updates, and invite or community activity, with push tokens stored per device.

Posts are temporary and deleted after their lifecycle ends, as described in our Privacy Policy. The Service is currently provided on an "as-is" beta basis, and we may modify, suspend, or discontinue any part of the Service at any time without notice.
