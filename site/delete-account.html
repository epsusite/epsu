Account and Data Deletion

Effective date: April 20, 2026. This page explains how to delete your Epsu account and what gets removed.

1. In-app deletion path

In the app, open Settings and choose Delete account. Epsu asks you to confirm your password before deletion.

2. If you cannot access the app

Email epsu.site@protonmail.com from the email address tied to your account and write Account deletion request in the subject line.

3. What deletion removes

Account deletion removes your auth account and profile-linked records, including notifications, reactions, reports filed by you, memberships, suggestions, school applications, subscriptions, push tokens, and account requests.

4. What may remain temporarily

Posts you already submitted are not immediately cascaded away with the account. Their author link is removed, and the posts stay only until their normal scheduled deletion cycle ends.

5. When deletion is blocked

You cannot delete your account while you still manage an Epsu. Resolve that management status first, then try again.
