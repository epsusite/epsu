What Epsu is

  • Anonymous posting for school and city communities
  • Public legal and support pages for trust and app review
  • Built-in moderation and account deletion flows

Identity-hidden

Users do not see who posted.

Anonymous to ordinary users, with backend accountability for moderation, safety, and deletion.

Place-based

Built around schools and regions.

Communities are tied to real institutions and locations instead of one giant generic feed.

Operational

Policies are not hidden.

Privacy, guidelines, deletion, and system behavior are documented publicly on this site.
