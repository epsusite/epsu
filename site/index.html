Epsu

Anonymous local communities

Your school and city, minus the names.

Epsu is built for local talk inside real communities. Posts stay identity-hidden to ordinary users, while moderation, privacy, and deletion rules stay public.

What Epsu is

  • Anonymous posting for school and city communities
  • Public legal and support pages for trust and app review
  • Built-in moderation and account deletion flows

Identity-hidden

Place-based

Operational
