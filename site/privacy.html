Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 20, 2026. This page explains what Epsu stores, why it stores it, and what choices and rights you may have.

1. Data we collect

Account data: email address, username, country code, notification preference, and timestamps showing acceptance of the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Community Guidelines.

Community data: Epsu memberships, roles, school applications, Epsu suggestions, moderator invites, owned or moderated Epsus, and uploaded school logos.

Content and safety data: posts, replies, reactions, reports, moderation actions, queued keyword flags, and in-app notifications.

Support and account data: account history, correction requests, account deletion requests you send us, and support emails.

2. Data stored on your device

The app keeps some data on your device too. That includes the login session, cached app data for faster startup, post drafts, the last push token, a notification prompt flag, and a pending moderator invite token.

3. How we use data

We use data to create and secure accounts, run Epsus, queue and release posts, let users react and report, process school applications, deliver notifications, review flagged or reported content, enforce rules, and provide account history and deletion tools.

4. Legal bases for GDPR users

Where GDPR or similar law applies, we generally rely on contract to provide the service you request, legitimate interests for security and moderation, consent for optional permissions such as push notifications or photo library access, and legal obligation where retention or disclosure is required by law.

5. Sharing and service providers

Epsu uses service providers to run the app. These currently include Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and server-side functions, Expo services for mobile delivery infrastructure including notifications, and Render for the public site. We do not describe this as selling your personal data.

6. Permissions

Epsu may request notification permission so it can deliver push notifications, and photo library permission only if you choose to upload a school logo while creating a school Epsu.

7. Moderation and keyword screening

Epsu checks posts against a keyword list before release. Flagged posts may go into a moderator queue before they become visible. Epsu also processes user reports, reaction signals, moderation actions, and 24-hour mutes.

8. Retention

Epsu is designed to keep community content short-lived. Posts are queued for hourly release and are deleted when their scheduled cycle expires. Reactions and reports attached to deleted posts are removed with those posts.

Read notifications and resolved or rejected account requests can also be purged after age-based cleanup.

Account profile data is generally kept until you delete the account, unless longer retention is required by law.

9. Account deletion

Epsu provides an in-app account deletion flow. Deleting an account removes the auth account and profile-linked records such as notifications, reactions, reports filed by that user, memberships, suggestions, school applications, subscriptions, push tokens, and account requests.

Because post authors are de-linked rather than deleted immediately, a still-live post you submitted may stay in the app without your account link until its normal deletion cycle ends.

You cannot delete your account while you still manage an Epsu.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, export, or object to certain processing. You may request help by emailing epsu.site@protonmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

11. Children

Epsu is not for children under 13. We collect date of birth during signup to apply age-based account rules.

12. Changes

We may update this Policy as the app changes. When we do, we will update the effective date on this page.

13. Contact

Privacy and data rights contact: epsu.site@protonmail.com.
